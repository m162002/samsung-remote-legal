Privacy Policy

Samsung TV Remote · Last updated: June 8, 2026 · Effective date: June 8, 2026
Language: English Русский

This Privacy Policy describes how the Samsung TV Remote mobile application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about what data the App processes.

Summary: The App does not collect personal information. All data needed to control your TV (local network identifiers, pairing tokens, preferences) is stored only on your device. The only third-party service used is Google AdMob for displaying advertisements.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To discover and control Samsung TVs on your local Wi-Fi network, the App processes and stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • TV network identifiers: the local IP address (e.g. 192.168.0.155), MAC address, and friendly name of the Samsung TV you choose to control.
  • Pairing tokens: short authentication tokens issued by your TV when you approve the App on the TV screen during the initial pairing.
  • App preferences: last-used TV, button layout settings, and language.

This information is stored in the App's private storage (Android SharedPreferences and Flutter Secure Storage). It is never transmitted to us or to any server we control. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifier
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or other content on your device
  • Your browsing history or activity in other apps
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Account credentials of any kind

1.3 Permissions Requested

The App requests only the minimum permissions required to function:

  • INTERNET — to communicate with your TV over Wi-Fi and to load ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect whether Wi-Fi is available
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to find devices on the same local network

The App does not request access to location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, SMS, or call logs.

2. How Information Is Used

The information stored locally is used solely to:

  • Discover Samsung TVs on the same Wi-Fi network as your device
  • Authenticate with the TV using the pairing token you approved
  • Send remote-control commands (volume, channels, navigation, power) to the TV
  • Remember your preferences so you do not have to pair again on each launch

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and process information as described in its own privacy policy, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate (city-level) location derived from IP
  • App usage information (ad impressions, clicks)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)

This data is used by Google for purposes including ad delivery, ad personalization, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Learn more in Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob & AdSense privacy disclosures

3.2 Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization", or reset your Advertising ID.
  • Within the App, on first launch you will be asked for consent regarding personalized ads (where required by law, e.g. EU, UK, Brazil).

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any data with third parties, except as required for the Google AdMob service described above.

We may disclose information only if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you:

  • Clear the App's data through Android Settings → Apps → Samsung TV Remote → Storage → Clear data
  • Uninstall the App

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us using the email below and we will take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

7.1 For All Users

You can fully remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

7.2 For Users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access information about how your data is processed
  • Request rectification of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for AdMob personalized ads, this is available through the in-app consent dialog)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

7.3 For California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. For AdMob's role in cross-context behavioral advertising, you can opt out via your device's Ad Settings as described in Section 3.2.

7.4 For Users in Other Jurisdictions

If you reside in Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), or other jurisdictions with similar privacy laws, equivalent rights apply. Contact us using the email below to exercise them.

8. Data Security

Locally stored data (in particular pairing tokens) is kept in Android's secure storage. Because the App communicates only with TVs on your own Wi-Fi network and does not transmit user data to our servers, the surface for remote attack is limited. We recommend keeping your phone and TV firmware up to date.

9. International Data Transfers

We do not transfer data internationally because we do not operate any servers that receive your data. Google AdMob may transfer data internationally as described in Google's privacy policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. For significant changes, we will notify users through an in-app notice on the next launch.
